What's it about?

Every night the boss picks a house. You get in, grab the goods, and make it back to the van before anyone notices. Every sound counts.

Online with friends

Host a room, send the 4-letter code, and you're in. Proximity voice chat: you only hear whoever is standing next to you. So does the homeowner.

Every house is different

Family homes, farmhouses, penthouses, haunted mansions, and every fifth night a big haul: a jewelry store, a mall, a museum or a casino.

Proper chaos

Throw your buddy across the room, pet the dog (30% chance it works), and watch out for the cat. Get knocked out and you float around as a ghost, BOO-ing the owner away.

Locks, safes, cameras

Pick locks with good timing, find the safe code, kill the power at the fuse box and loop the cameras. Carpet is quiet, tile is loud.

Cases and rare loot

Open CS2-style cases and collect over 100 cosmetics. The rarest hats come with TF2-style unusual effects: flames, money rain, ghosts.

Progression and accounts

Reputation, new roles (safecracker, driver, con artist), challenges and trophies. Log in and your save follows you between Mac, Windows and phone.

Installing

The game isn't signed with Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks the first time you open it. You only have to do this once.

Mac (M1 or newer)

  1. Download the .dmg file and open it.
  2. Drag Hush Money into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll down, click Open Anyway and confirm. You only have to do this once.
  5. Updating? Drag the old Hush Money from Applications to the Trash first. Your progress stays.

Windows 10 / 11

  1. Download the setup.exe file and run it.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.
  4. Follow the installer, then launch it from the Start menu.

Browser

  1. Open the game in your browser.
  2. If nobody has played in a while, the first load can take up to a minute while the server wakes up.
  3. Controls: WASD move, E grab, left click shove, Shift sprint.

For voice chat, allow the microphone when the game asks. It's only used in-game with your crew, and whatever you say, the homeowner can "hear" too.
